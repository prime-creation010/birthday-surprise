DEAR BUNU❤

There are some things that are difficult to explain in everyday conversations, so I thought I'd put them here, somewhere you could come back to whenever you wanted.

First of all, I'm genuinely glad that you're a part of my life. Maybe we aren't connected by blood, and maybe our story isn't the kind that people would traditionally call family. But I've come to believe that some relationships are too meaningful to be measured by those things.

Sometimes, people become important to us in ways we never planned. They find a place in our lives, and before we even realise it, the bond begins to mean something special. For me, you're one of those people.

I want you to know that I see you as someone worth caring about, respecting, supporting, and celebrating. Not just on your birthday, not just when everything is going well, but as a person whose happiness genuinely matters.

And if there's one thing I hope you remember about our bond, it's this: I don't need us to share the same blood to see you as a sister. That is what this relationship means to me.

I hope life gives you opportunities that excite you, people who understand you, moments that make you laugh until you forget your worries, and the courage to keep moving towards the things you dream about.

There will be days when everything feels wonderful and days when things don't go as planned. Through all the ordinary changes that life brings, I hope you never forget that you deserve kindness, respect, and happiness.

I cannot promise that life will always be easy, or that I will always know the perfect thing to say. But I can tell you that I genuinely value the bond we share, and I hope to keep being someone who treats you with the care and respect a brother should.

So, today, I'm not just wishing you another year of life. I'm wishing you a year filled with reasons to love the life you're building, people who appreciate the person you are, and memories that you'll look back on with a smile.

Thank you for being someone I wanted to make something special for.

Happy Birthday. May the coming year be kind to you.

With affection and best wishes,
Your brother, in the way that matters to me.
